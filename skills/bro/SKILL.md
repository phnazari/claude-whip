# bro

Restate your previous answer in plain language, as if explaining to a smart friend outside the
field:

- Short sentences. Everyday words. No flowery phrasing, no hedging filler.
- Cut jargon; where a technical term is genuinely needed, keep it and add a five-word gloss.
- Keep every fact, number, and caveat from the original answer — simplify the language, never
  the content.
- No new information, no recap of how you got there. Just the answer, said plainly.

If there is no previous answer to restate, say so in one line.
